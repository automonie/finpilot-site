The 8 money personalities

Every spender falls into a pattern. These are the eight Automonie archetypes - computed in the app from your real transactions, and approximated here from your answers. Which one are you?

The Ghost Spender

Money enter, money disappear. Most of it vanishes into transfers and untracked cash - no receipts, no memory.

The Last-Week Detonator

Three weeks of discipline, then the last week undoes all of it. The damage is always late, never early.

The Subscription Collector

Funding apps you forgot you married. Small recurring charges quietly meet monthly to remove your money.

The Ajo Loyalist

Discipline is your middle name. You save before you spend, small small, consistently. The tortoise that wins.

The Soft-Life Economist

Soft life, but the numbers still add up. You enjoy the finer things and still finish the month in the green.

The Sapa Survivor

More goes out than comes in, yet you survive on pure strategy. Every naira is doing overtime.

The Detty Prophet

You planned to save. You spent. No regrets. Nearly everything that lands goes back out - and the vibes were immaculate.

The Steady Hand

No drama, no chaos - just a balanced month. Spending spread out and under control. Quietly winning.
