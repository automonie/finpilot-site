What does it cost to live in Lagos or Abuja?

There is no single number - your rent, your area and your lifestyle move the total by hundreds of thousands of naira. The calculator gives you a sensible starting point by city and lifestyle, then lets you edit every line so the total is actually yours.

Rent - the biggest lever

Rent swings the widest. A self-contained flat on the mainland is a different world from a serviced one-bed in Lekki or Maitama. It is usually the single largest line, so it is also where a move saves the most.

Food, transport & the quiet drains

Food and transport are the lines people underestimate most, followed by data and subscriptions. Individually small, together they often rival rent - which is exactly why seeing them added up is a wake-up call.

Know your number, then track against it

An estimate is a target. The real value comes from checking it against what you actually spent - that gap is where the surprises (and the savings) live. That is the job Automonie does automatically from your bank statements and alerts.
